Room by room

Inside every clean

Here’s how a visit breaks down — the same thorough approach whether it’s a standard clean or a full deep clean.

Kitchen

Counters and surfaces wiped down, sink refreshed, and floors vacuumed and mopped — with oven and fridge interiors available as add-ons.

Bathrooms

Showers, tubs, toilets and fixtures detailed, mirrors polished, and grout given proper attention on deep cleans.

Bedrooms & living areas

Whole-home dusting, vacuuming and mopping, with baseboards, blinds and behind-furniture areas covered on deep cleans.

Good to know

Questions, answered

New to booking a cleaner? Here’s what Largo homeowners usually ask us first.

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies?

Yes — every visit includes our own professional-grade products and equipment, and we arrive in our own transport. There’s nothing for you to provide or stock.

How is my price worked out?

Every home is different, so your final quote is tailored to your home’s size and condition. The packages on this site are examples to show what’s possible — call or send the quote form and we’ll price your space properly, free and with no pressure.

Can I book a regular cleaning schedule?

Absolutely. Recurring plans run weekly, bi-weekly or monthly, so your home stays consistently fresh without you having to think about it. You can also book one-off standard, deep or move-in/move-out cleans.

Do you clean offices and rentals too?

Yes — we clean offices, rental properties and small businesses in the Largo area, during the day or after hours, on the schedule you set.

Call (727) 612-8739, email applesweetcleaningservices@gmail.com, or send the quote form on our contact page. Tell us about your space and we’ll get back to you with a tailored quote.
